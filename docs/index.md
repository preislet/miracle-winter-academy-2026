## Two tracks: Basic and Advanced

Lectures and two practicals are shared by everyone. Most hands-on workshops run in two parallel
rooms, one per track. On every workshop page you can switch between the <span class="pill basic">Basic</span>
and <span class="pill advanced">Advanced</span> tab; your choice is remembered across pages.

## What you will learn

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-source-branch: **Version control** with Git as a safety net for working with AI agents
-   :material-language-r: **R and Python** for reading, running and reviewing analysis code
-   :material-robot-outline: **AI-assisted coding and science**: instructing coding agents, sandboxing, permissions, MCP, checking results critically
-   :material-scatter-plot: **Single-cell and flow data**: from matrices and PCA to clustering and integration
-   :material-database-check: **Imperfect data**: curation, imputation and statistics in practice
-   :material-shield-lock-outline: **Working responsibly with medical data**: GDPR, FAIR, AI Act, MDR

</div>
