## Lecture abstracts

**Opening lecture: Natural history of CLIP and how we do MRD in paediatric AML**
: *Jan Trka* · Mon 09:30

**Antigen Receptor Analysis in Hematological Malignancies: Clonality, MRD and Repertoire. Methods and Pitfalls**
: *Eva Froňková* · Mon 10:00

**Science in the Age of Agentic AI: A Practical Perspective**
: *Jan Stuchlý* · Mon 11:00

**Computational foundations of single-cell data analysis**
: *Jan Stuchlý* · Tue 09:00
: Vector and matrix representations of single-cell data; distances, matrix factorisation (PCA, NMF) and dimensionality reduction; interpretation and limitations of reduced-dimensional representations.

**Clonal origin and evolution in BCR::ABL1-positive leukaemias: lessons learned from MRD**
: *Jan Zuna* · Tue 16:15
: Insights from MRD monitoring into the clonal origin and evolution of BCR::ABL1-positive leukaemias.

**Working responsibly with medical data: privacy, FAIR, AI risk and regulation**
: *John Jacobs* · Wed 13:30
: Medical data privacy and governance (GDPR, FAIR principles); AI risks and regulation in medicine (AI Act, MDR); model validation and reproducibility.

**New trends in the treatment of paediatric ALL: is the era of immunotherapy coming?**
: *Lucie Šrámková* · Wed 15:50
: Current role of immunotherapy in the treatment of paediatric ALL.

**Selected topics from statistics**
: *Jan Stuchlý* · Thu 09:00
: Probability and statistical reasoning in data analysis; worked examples in R.

**Computational analysis of single-cell and flow data**
: *Costa Bachas* · Thu 13:20
: Single-cell and flow cytometry data: acquisition, preprocessing and modelling of cell populations with Gaussian mixtures.
