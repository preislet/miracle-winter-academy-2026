Motol University Hospital, where the Second Faculty of Medicine, Charles University is based.

## From the airport

Take bus **119** from Václav Havel Airport to **Nádraží Veleslavín**, then metro **A** two stops
towards Nemocnice Motol. Allow about 30–40 minutes in total.

## Tickets

Public transport tickets are valid on metro, trams and buses. Buy them in the
[PID Lítačka app](https://pid.cz/en/tickets/) or from ticket machines in the metro stations.
